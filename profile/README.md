## One brand, connected tools

Shoal builds tools for the people who create software and the people who run digital businesses. Developers need a practical way to ship and operate applications. Business teams need clear reporting and less repetitive work.

Our aim is an all-in-one ecosystem that connects those needs. Each product solves a specific problem and can stand on its own, so you can start with what you need rather than adopt an entire stack.

## For developers

We focus on software delivery, infrastructure and automation that small teams can understand and control.

[Shoal Orchestrator](https://github.com/Shoal-Technology/shoal) is our open-source container orchestrator, built in Go with containerd and an embedded web portal. It is currently in alpha. Check the repository for installation instructions, tested capabilities and limitations before using it in production.

## For digital businesses

We focus on everyday operations, reporting and the handoff between technical work and business decisions.

[Shoal Pulse](https://shoal-technology.solutions/pulse/) is our client reporting product. Its current CSV beta preview lets you explore fictional brands and reports through a guest demo. Public account access is not open yet.

## What we are building toward

The wider Shoal direction includes AI-assisted development, connected automation and business workflows. These are development directions, not a claim that every capability is available today. We publish each product's actual scope and release status separately.
